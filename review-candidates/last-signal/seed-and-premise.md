# Seed and Premise Tournament

## Seed

A night-shift lighthouse keeper begins receiving weather reports from the
station that will replace hers thirty years in the future.

## Haunt

What if the future that makes you obsolete still depends on the truth you
buried?

## Premise Tournament

1. **The Forecast Debt** - A keeper sells future forecasts to fishermen until
   one predicts her own death. Strong urgency, but the moral choice narrows to
   self-preservation.
2. **The Empty Frequency** - A retired radio operator hears distress calls from
   ships erased from official records. Atmospheric, but causality depends on
   several unrelated mysteries.
3. **The Last Signal** - During her station's final storm, a keeper receives a
   future warning whose accuracy depends on correcting the fatal log she
   falsified decades earlier. The external rescue and internal confession are
   one action.
4. **Lantern for Tomorrow** - A child in a drowned city talks to the keeper
   through the lamp. Emotionally direct, but the time mechanism risks becoming
   a rescue shortcut.
5. **The Automated Saint** - A lighthouse AI asks its keeper to let one vessel
   wreck so thousands can be modeled and saved. High stakes, but less intimate
   and more dependent on abstract calculation.

## Selection

Candidate 3 wins because the climax can force a costly, irreversible choice:
Mara must sacrifice the respectable identity she built after her brother's
death in order to make both the present rescue and the future warning possible.
