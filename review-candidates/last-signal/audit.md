# Critic Audit

## Structural Audit

Pass with revisions. The external line is causal: future warning leads to
testing, archive discovery, confession, correction, lamp action, and ferry
turn. The first draft compresses Ivo's change of behavior and makes the archive
correction appear too mechanically sufficient.

## Crisis and Climax Audit

Pass. The crisis preserves two incompatible goods: Mara's earned security and
public identity versus truthful action that gives eleven people their best
chance. The climax grows from her choice and requires both confession and
physical action. Revision must prevent Ivo's release of the key from reading as
permission that reduces Mara's agency.

## Character and Arc Audit

Pass with revisions. Mara moves from maintenance-as-concealment to
maintenance-as-accountability. Ivo functions as credible institutional
opposition, not a villain. Add a specific passenger voice or object so the
ferry's lives are not only a number.

## Scene, Gap, and Subtext Audit

Pass with revisions. Every scene turns. Strongest gap: Mara expects her spotless
record to support her warning, but Ivo's praise makes the hidden lie more
dangerous. Dialogue is restrained, though the draft states the pension threat
too directly and should let Ivo's procedural language carry more subtext.

## Cliche, Specificity, and Voice Audit

No blocking cliche. The future printer and last lighthouse are familiar
materials, but the archive-training dependency and confession mechanism combine
them specifically. Add operational texture to the ferry approach and vary
repeated abstract words such as "certified" and "archive."

## Continuity and Setup-Payoff Audit

- Disconnected printer: explained by the shared copper ground and storm path.
- Brass key: introduced in handover context, paid off in crisis.
- Hand crank: introduced by inventory, paid off at climax.
- Carbon copy: image motif and evidence object.
- 03:17 warning: paid off by the lamp sweep.
- Consequence: inquiry remains active; no instant absolution.

## Findings
