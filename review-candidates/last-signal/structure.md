# Structure, Acts, Scenes, and Beats

## Spine

- Inciting incident: a printer marked for disposal produces a forecast dated
  2056: `KEEP KESTREL LIT 03:17. BELLWEATHER.`
- Progressive complications: the forecast predicts local failures; Ivo orders
  shutdown; Mara discovers the future model inherited a blank interval from her
  1996 log; Bellweather loses its offshore beacon.
- Crisis: preserve retirement by obeying shutdown and allow the ferry to trust
  automation, or confess the falsified log and illegally relight the lamp.
- Climax: Mara broadcasts the corrected log and turns the Fresnel lens at
  03:17; Bellweather turns before the submerged breakwater.
- Resolution: the future message confirms arrival and corrected archive. Mara
  faces inquiry without reclaiming her former innocence.

## Act and Sequence Design

### Act I - Extinguishing

1. **Inventory:** Mara prepares the station for automation. Value turns from
   controlled farewell to intrusion when the future warning prints.
2. **Test:** Two minor predictions prove exact. Value turns from skepticism to
   credible danger.

### Act II - The Blank Line

3. **Authority:** Ivo rejects the message and orders physical disconnection.
   Value turns from possible help to institutional isolation.
4. **Archive:** Mara traces the model's checksum to the 1996 storm record and
   her falsified blank interval. Value turns from mystery to culpability.
5. **Approach:** Bellweather reports failed navigation aids while the future
   warning degrades. Value turns from abstract risk to eleven lives.
6. **Crisis:** Ivo arrives with the shutdown key. Mara must choose public truth
   and unlawful action or private safety and obedience.

### Act III - Illumination

7. **Climax:** Mara confesses over the recorded channel, corrects the archive,
   and lights the lens. Bellweather turns.
8. **Resolution:** the future confirms the changed result; dawn and inquiry
   arrive together.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finish inventory cleanly | Printer refuses to stay dead | Obsolete equipment knows a future event | Warning names the ferry | Lamp crank and shutdown key set up |
| 2 | Disprove warning | Predictions become exact | Rational tests increase danger | Mara calls Ivo | Copper ground and salt set up |
| 3 | Keep station available | Ivo prioritizes protocol | Her spotless record is used against her | He invokes 1996 commendation | Reputation becomes a weapon |
| 4 | Find message source | Archive exposes missing data | The warning depends on her lie | She recovers Tomas's true call time | Blank line and carbon copy pay off |
| 5 | Warn Bellweather indirectly | Captain trusts certified systems | Warning without confession lacks authority | Beacon fails | Eleven passengers become concrete |
| 6 | Keep lamp and conceal guilt | Ivo arrives with shutdown key | No compromise preserves both goods | Mara opens recorded channel | Pension and key pay off |
| 7 | Save ferry through truthful action | Storm, Ivo, seized mechanism | Confession alone is insufficient | Corrected archive unlocks final bearing | Crank, lens, and true time pay off |
| 8 | Learn whether choice mattered | No immediate absolution | Rescue does not erase consequence | Future archive confirms correction | Carbon ghost becomes clean line |
