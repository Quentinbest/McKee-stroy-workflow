# Story Contract

## Premise Card

During the last night before Kestrel Head lighthouse is automated, keeper Mara
Venn receives a warning from its future replacement. To save an inbound ferry,
she must publicly correct the falsified storm log that secured her career and
concealed her part in her brother's death.

## Genre Contract

- Primary: contained speculative mystery.
- Secondary: moral drama and maritime survival.
- Obligatory moments: impossible signal; rational testing; hidden causal link;
  escalating storm; no-good crisis choice; visible maritime climax; answer that
  preserves wonder without erasing consequence.
- Avoid: chosen-one mythology, consequence-free time travel, a future machine
  that solves the present, and a confession rewarded with instant forgiveness.

## Controlling Idea

Responsibility restores a future when truth is acted upon at the cost of the
identity built to conceal it.

Counter-idea: systems are safest when private failures remain buried.

## Cast System

- **Mara Venn, 61, keeper:** wants an honorable retirement and her full pension.
  She needs to accept that maintenance without truth is another form of decay.
- **Ivo Chen, 38, district engineer:** wants a clean automated handover. He
  pressures Mara with policy, evidence standards, and the pension she can lose.
- **Nia Holt, 44, captain of the ferry Bellweather:** wants to deliver eleven
  island residents before the harbor closes. Her practical radio calls turn the
  mystery into an immediate human cost.
- **Tomas Venn, deceased:** Mara's younger brother. His absence is active
  opposition because Mara has converted guilt into professional perfection.
- **Kestrel Array, 2056:** a future forecast system trained on archived station
  logs. It can send terse forecast bursts but cannot act in 2026.

## World Bible

Kestrel Head stands above a basalt harbor on Bellwether Island. A mechanical
Fresnel lamp remains operable during the final handover, but policy requires it
dark once the offshore LED beacon enters service. The old weather printer uses
a copper ground shared with the archive sensor. Saltwater intrusion during an
exceptional storm creates a brief, unexplained data path between the 2026
printer and the 2056 array. The path transfers text only.

## Voice and Image Anchors

Close third person through Mara. Concrete maritime vocabulary, restrained
emotion, and short technical dialogue under pressure. Recurring images:
carbon-paper ghosts, salt blooming on metal, the lens as an eye, and blank log
lines. The key image is the old lamp turning across rain while Mara's confession
travels on the same circuit.
